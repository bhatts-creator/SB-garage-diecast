Blinkit / Stores nearby / Trips abroad

Always room for one more.

I’m obsessed with cars. Diecast cars are how that obsession has taken over my shelves. The collection has grown pretty big, but somehow there’s always another model I want to add.

The best bit is finding it. Checking Blinkit, looking through stores nearby, or keeping an eye out while travelling abroad. Spotting something you’ve been hunting for—or a car you didn’t know you needed—is half the fun.

SB Garage is where I share that collection and let a few finds make their way to someone else’s shelf. If one car has ever turned into “just one more” for you, you’ll feel at home here.
